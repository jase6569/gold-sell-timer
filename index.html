Get alerts on your phone

The app checks gold every hour, even when your phone is off. It sends you a notification when:

  1. gold moves into the sell zone (sell a portion), or the strong sell zone
  2. gold is getting close to the sell zone, so you can get ready
  3. the sell zone ends
  4. gold starts or stops a steep slide, which changes how much stock to hold if you buy in

You also get a short summary every Friday evening after the market closes.

How to read it

  1. The white line is the gold price per gram.
  2. The shaded gold area is where it's worth selling. When the white line climbs into it, the app tells you to sell, in stages.

How to use Gold Trader

Gold Trader is built for a gold dealer. You buy gold in every day, and your profit is the gap between what you pay the customer and what the refiner pays you. Every gram sitting in the safe is a bet on the gold price, so the app answers three questions: how much stock should I keep, what should I send to the refiner this week, and what should I pay today? When the answer changes, it pings your phone.

The short version
  1. Work through Start here at the top of this page: home screen, alerts, and joining the team.
  2. In the Shop's Safe, under Your gold, tell it roughly how much you buy in a month (Send tab), then add any gold you hold that didn't come through the Buy log.
  3. Check the top of the Advice page, or wait for an alert. Your stock: what to do this week tells you how much to keep and how many grams to send, oldest gold first.
  4. Book visits in the Shop's Diary and use Route for my visits today on the day. At the visit, tap Make an offer, show the customer, and if they accept the Buy log opens with everything filled in. Take the ID photo and let the app read the details, add the photos and signature, and save.

4. What to do with your stock

Steep slide: don't build up stock
Gold is more than 10% below its 40-week average and falling. Only two slides like this since 2004: 2013 kept falling another 10–15%, 2021 recovered. Too uncertain to sit on stock.
Keep about 1 month's buying, send the rest
Fine to hold stock
Gold isn't stretched or sliding. From here gold was lower 6 months later only about 26–30% of the time.
Hold up to your limit, send the oldest first
Sell zone
Gold is stretched well above normal, so it's expensive.
Sell stock down to about 1 month, at least ¼
Strong sell zone
Gold was lower 6 months later 87% of the time from here.
Clear most of your stock, keep a small reserve

Why sell in stages? Gold often climbs further after first entering the sell zone. In October 2025 it entered at £93/g, ran up to £125/g in January, then dropped back. Selling a part at each stage means you never sell everything too early, and never hold everything through a fall.

Selling into a sudden drop isn't part of the plan. In past UK prices, new 3-month lows were more often followed by a bounce than a further fall, so the app never tells you to panic-sell on a dip. It just stops you piling up stock in a long slide.

5. Out today, Coins and your gold

Out today is for home visits, linked live to the Shop. It shows your visits for today with Call, Text (a reminder asking them to have photo ID ready), Directions, Leaving now, Make an offer, Start the purchase and No sale, plus a route for the whole day. Clock in, Going out and I'm back keep the Shop's "who's where" right, and the Shop warns the others if you're not back 30 minutes after your time. Under that: what to pay today per carat, and the cash you're carrying (purchases paid from your own cash come off automatically). To book or move a visit, use the Shop's Diary.

Your gold (what you hold, how much to send and which lots first, adding gold, lot alerts and the refiner price) is in the Shop, on the Safe page, next to the bags it's in. The Advice page still tells you what to do with your stock this week.

Coins. Gold and silver coins, three ways:

  1. Look up a coin. Pick it from the list to see its weight, size, gold or silver content, melt value, what the refiner pays and what to pay.
  2. Identify a coin. Weigh it, measure across it if you can, and pick its colour. It matches against the official mint specs and warns you if it's too heavy, too light or the wrong size, which are classic signs of a fake.
  3. Scan with camera. Photograph a coin, a handful of coins or a whole set in its case, up to 4 photos (front, back, certificate). It lists every coin and the set, then totals the pure gold and silver, melt value, what the refiner pays and what to pay, using the official weights. Then weigh them in the box below: it tells you if the weight matches. Anything marked check it needs a closer look.

Proof coins, complete boxed sets and old coins are often worth more than melt, and the app says don't scrap when that's likely. A photo can't prove a coin is real, so always weigh before you buy.

6. The Buy log

Every purchase from a customer is recorded in the Buy log. Open it from Shop, Buy (or from a visit in the Shop's Diary). Every purchase either of you makes is shared, so you can both look up any customer. Each phone has its own PIN lock in Settings.

  1. Don't melt this. Coins, medals, designer, signed, antique and stone-set pieces are flagged with a yellow Don't melt this? note on the offer screen and in the Buy log, because they usually sell for more as they are than as scrap. Tick Keep it: don't melt and the piece is kept back: it shows under Kept back, don't melt on the Send tab, isn't counted in your stock to send, and never goes in a refiner batch. You can tick it for gold you add by hand under Your gold too.
  2. New purchase. Take the ID front photo, then tap Fill in the details from the ID photo: the app reads the name, date of birth, ID number, expiry and address into any empty boxes. Always check them against the ID. Then the seller's details, their photo ID and proof of address (with photos), each item (description, carat, weight, price, photos on the scales and of the hallmark), how you paid, and the seller's signature on the declaration. Saving it gives a receipt number and adds the gold to your gold list for you.
  3. Records. Search by name, ID number, postcode, phone, item or receipt number. Open a record to email the receipt to the customer. You can print a police report for a date range, or export a spreadsheet (CSV). Receipt numbers carry the buyer's initial, for example DG-20261122-J001 for Jase and DG-20261122-M001 for Mike.
  4. Google reviews. Each record has a Google review card: show the customer a QR code to scan while you're with them, or ask by text, WhatsApp or email, then tap They've left a review. The Records page shows who's still to ask, and nobody is asked twice once they've reviewed. Add the review link once, in Settings.
  5. Settings. The business details for receipts and reports, the Google review link (both shared), plus your own staff name, PIN lock and backups.

Back up every week. One locked backup covers everything: the Shop, the Buy log with all its photos, your gold, the diary, rota and cash. Tap Back up everything in the Shop (or Back up now in the Buy log): it opens the Shop app, where you lock it with a password and save it to Google Drive. Restore it from the Shop app, Settings.

Data protection. Because you keep customers' names, addresses, ID and photos, the business needs to pay the ICO's yearly data protection fee (small businesses pay the lowest tier) and offer sellers a privacy notice. The Shop's Settings, Data protection has the ICO link and the privacy notice to print or show the customer. It says what you record, why, who sees it, how long you keep it (your retention setting) and their rights. It's also added to the bottom of every emailed receipt.

9. How the advice works

The stock call looks at one thing: how far gold has stretched above or fallen below its 40-week average, and whether it has started to turn. That was the only measure that worked reliably when tested on 22 years of UK gold prices (2004 to today), and it held up in both 2005–2015 and 2016–2026. Popular rules like "RSI over 70" or "near its high" didn't hold up for gold, so they're not used. News is shown for background and timing only, because it can't be tested the same way.

The sell zone prices move slowly as the 40-week average changes, so check them now and then rather than writing them down once.

Questions

The price is different from my refiner's. The top of the Advice page shows the market (spot) price. Every "what you'd be paid" figure uses the refiner price set in the Shop (Safe, Your gold, Alerts & prices): Mastermelt's daily counter price, or your own %.

The Mastermelt figure doesn't move during the day. That's right. Mastermelt sets its counter price once a day, so it changes when they update it. The live value above it moves every minute.

No signal? The app still opens, with your stock call, Out today, coins and the Buy log. It shows the last live price your phone saw, with the time, and updates as soon as you're back online. Anything you add with no signal is saved on the phone and shared with the team when the signal returns. Only the coin scanner needs a signal. Open the app once with signal after an update so the phone keeps the newest copy.

It says "Live feed unavailable". The app is showing the last saved price and keeps retrying every minute. The advice still works.

I haven't had an alert for a while. That's normal: alerts come when the stock call changes, plus the Friday summary. If even the Friday summary stops, check you're still subscribed in ntfy and that notifications for it aren't muted.

The ID reader filled in something wrong, or nothing at all. It only fills boxes that are empty and can misread a worn or shiny card, so always check every detail against the ID itself before saving. If it says the photo is hard to read, retake it flat, in good light, with the whole card filling the frame. It never decides whether an ID is genuine: that's your check.

The offer screen says it's waiting for the price. It needs today's gold price, so check your signal. Offers use the price at the time, so if you go back to a customer another day, make a new offer.

The route opens in the wrong order. It follows appointment times, so add a time to each visit. Visits without a time go last, and only your own visits (or ones marked "either of us") are included.

The scan says it couldn't read the coins. Lay them flat, in good light, close enough that the dates are clear, and try again. Each scan costs a fraction of a penny.

I've got a new phone. Install the app on it and join the team again with the passphrase: everything comes back down. Then set your Buy log PIN and staff name again, and subscribe to the alert code in ntfy. If you weren't in the team, use Back up now on the old phone and Restore backup in the Buy log Settings on the new one.

Something the other person added isn't showing. Open the Shop's Settings, Team on both devices and tap Share now. If it says changes are waiting, that phone hasn't had signal yet. Changes normally show within a minute while the app is open.

We both changed the same thing. The most recent change wins for that item, for example one visit or one lot of gold. Different items never overwrite each other, so you can both work at the same time.

The widget won't install. Uninstall the old widget app first (press and hold the Gold Trader icon, tap App info, then Uninstall), then install the new one from the Alerts page.

Is it always right? No. It's based on what has happened before, and big world events can change things fast. It's a strong guide, not a guarantee, and it isn't regulated financial advice. The decision is always yours.
