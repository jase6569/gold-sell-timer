Get sell alerts on your phone

The app checks gold every hour, even when your phone is off. It sends you a notification when:

  1. gold moves into the sell zone (sell a portion), or the strong sell zone

How to use Gold Trader

Gold Trader is built for a gold dealer. You buy gold in every day, and your profit is the gap between what you pay the customer and what the refiner pays you. Every gram sitting in the safe is a bet on the gold price, so the app answers three questions: how much stock should I keep, what should I send to the refiner this week, and what should I pay today? When the answer changes, it pings your phone.

The short version
  1. Work through Start here at the top of this page: home screen, alerts, and joining the team.
  2. On My gold, tell it roughly how much you buy in a month (Send tab), then add the gold you're holding with what you paid (My stock tab).
  3. Check the top of the Advice page, or wait for an alert. Your stock: what to do this week tells you how much to keep and how many grams to send, oldest gold first.
  4. Book visits in Team, Diary. Before you buy, check Make money for what to pay per gram, then tap Start the purchase on the visit to record it in the Buy log.

4. What to do with your stock

Steep slide: don't build up stock
Gold is more than 10% below its 40-week average and falling. Only two slides like this since 2004: 2013 kept falling another 10–15%, 2021 recovered. Too uncertain to sit on stock.
Keep about 1 month's buying, send the rest
Fine to hold stock
Gold isn't stretched or sliding. From here gold was lower 6 months later only about 26–30% of the time.
Hold up to your limit, send the oldest first
Sell zone
Gold is stretched well above normal, so it's expensive.
Sell stock down to about 1 month, at least ¼
Strong sell zone
Gold was lower 6 months later 87% of the time from here.
Clear most of your stock, keep a small reserve

Why sell in stages? Gold often climbs further after first entering the sell zone. In October 2025 it entered at £93/g, ran up to £125/g in January, then dropped back. Selling a part at each stage means you never sell everything too early, and never hold everything through a fall.

Selling into a sudden drop isn't part of the plan. In past UK prices, new 3-month lows were more often followed by a bounce than a further fall, so the app never tells you to panic-sell on a dip. It just stops you piling up stock in a long slide.

5. My gold, tab by tab

Once you've joined the team, My gold is shared: your stock, what you've bought and sent, and the buying-in, margin and refiner settings are the same on both phones, and a change on one shows on the other within a minute. Before you join, it's saved on your phone only. Adam's app never sees any of it.

Overview. Your week (this week and last): what you bought and your margin on the refiner's price that day, what you sent to the refiner and roughly what you made, and what you're still holding. Under it, the live value of all your gold. If you haven't backed up for 7 days, a reminder shows here too.

My stock. Add each piece or batch you buy: weight, carat, what you paid and the date. My lots groups it by week or month and shows each lot's break-even price and profit, plus which lots to sell first. Gold bought through the Buy log is added here for you.

Send. Buying in: enter roughly how many grams you buy in a month, and the most stock you want to hold (start with 2 months). It then shows how many grams of each carat to send to the refiner this week, oldest first, and roughly what you'll be paid. When the batch has gone, tap I've sent this batch so next week's figures are right. If a big announcement is due, a timing tip tells you to wait for it.

Make money. Shows once you've joined the team (or the Buy log is switched on for your phone). The margin you set here is shared, so you both offer customers the same price per gram:

  1. What to pay today. Set the margin you want to keep (e.g. 20%) and it shows the most to pay per gram for each carat, from the refiner's price. Check it before each purchase, especially while gold is falling.
  2. Lots to send now. Each lot marked Send now (only just above break-even, so a bad week would turn it into a loss), Lock it in (in profit and gold is in the sell zone), Safe for now, or At a loss (with the price it breaks even at).
  3. How well you're buying. Your margin month by month: what you paid against what the refiner was paying the day you bought. Green means you hit your target.
  4. Lot alerts. Type in your alert code and tap Turn on lot alerts. The app tells you where the lot alerts will arrive. If it shows a lot channel code, subscribe to that code in ntfy too. Only the month, grams, carats and what you paid are sent, locked so only your own alert check can read them. Customer details never leave the phone.

Coins. Gold and silver coins, three ways:

  1. Look up a coin. Pick it from the list to see its weight, size, gold or silver content, melt value, what the refiner pays and what to pay.
  2. Identify a coin. Weigh it, measure across it if you can, and pick its colour. It matches against the official mint specs and warns you if it's too heavy, too light or the wrong size, which are classic signs of a fake.
  3. Scan with camera. Photograph a coin, a handful of coins or a whole set in its case, up to 4 photos (front, back, certificate). It lists every coin and the set, then totals the pure gold and silver, melt value, what the refiner pays and what to pay, using the official weights. Then weigh them in the box below: it tells you if the weight matches. Anything marked check it needs a closer look.

Proof coins, complete boxed sets and old coins are often worth more than melt, and the app says don't scrap when that's likely. A photo can't prove a coin is real, so always weigh before you buy.

Prices. Refiner prices sets the price used for every "what you'd be paid" figure in the app. Mastermelt trade counter prices (the default) are read automatically each day, so the figures match Mastermelt. If you get a different rate, choose My own % and type it in. Below that, Quick check values any single piece by weight and carat.

9. How the advice works

The stock call looks at one thing: how far gold has stretched above or fallen below its 40-week average, and whether it has started to turn. That was the only measure that worked reliably when tested on 22 years of UK gold prices (2004 to today), and it held up in both 2005–2015 and 2016–2026. Popular rules like "RSI over 70" or "near its high" didn't hold up for gold, so they're not used. News is shown for background and timing only, because it can't be tested the same way.

The sell zone prices move slowly as the 40-week average changes, so check them now and then rather than writing them down once.

Questions

The price is different from my refiner's. The top of the Advice page shows the market (spot) price. Every "what you'd be paid" figure uses the refiner price from My gold, Prices: Mastermelt's daily counter price, or your own %.

The Mastermelt figure doesn't move during the day. That's right. Mastermelt sets its counter price once a day, so it changes when they update it. The live value above it moves every minute.

It says "Live feed unavailable". The app is showing the last saved price and keeps retrying every minute. The advice still works.

I haven't had an alert for a while. That's normal: alerts come when the stock call changes, plus the Friday summary. If even the Friday summary stops, check you're still subscribed in ntfy and that notifications for it aren't muted.

The scan says it couldn't read the coins. Lay them flat, in good light, close enough that the dates are clear, and try again. Each scan costs a fraction of a penny.

I've got a new phone. Install the app on it and join the team again with the passphrase: everything comes back down. Then set your Buy log PIN and staff name again, and subscribe to the alert code in ntfy. If you weren't in the team, use Back up now on the old phone and Restore backup in the Buy log Settings on the new one.

Something the other person added isn't showing. Open Team, Sharing on both phones and tap Share now. If it says changes are waiting, that phone hasn't had signal yet. Changes normally show within a minute while the app is open.

We both changed the same thing. The most recent change wins for that item, for example one visit or one lot of gold. Different items never overwrite each other, so you can both work at the same time.

The widget won't install. Uninstall the old widget app first (press and hold the Gold Trader icon, tap App info, then Uninstall), then install the new one from the Alerts page.

Is it always right? No. It's based on what has happened before, and big world events can change things fast. It's a strong guide, not a guarantee, and it isn't regulated financial advice. The decision is always yours.
