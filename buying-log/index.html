Data protection

You keep customers' names, addresses, ID and photos, so the business needs to be registered with the ICO (the UK's data protection regulator), and customers have a right to know how their details are used.

  1. Check whether you need to pay, and pay, at ico.org.uk. Small businesses pay the tier 1 fee once a year.
  2. Offer every seller the privacy notice. Tap below to show it on your phone, or send it with the receipt (it's added to every emailed receipt automatically).
