Give each person their own PIN. Whoever signs in is recorded on every sale, payment, bag and change they make. Managers approve refunds, cancelled sales, deleted stock, putting back old versions and removing ID.

Back up

Saves all sales, stock, customers (with their ID photos), the till and the safe log to one file. It holds customers' ID, so keep it somewhere private. Purchase records have their own backup in the Buy log's Settings.
